BabyLink Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to [support e-mail]. I usually answer within a few days. Please include your iPhone models and iOS versions.

What you need

  • Two or more iPhones with BabyLink installed: one is the Camera, the others are Parents (up to 4).
  • All devices on the same Wi-Fi network, or connected to one iPhone’s Personal Hotspot.
  • “Local Network” allowed for BabyLink on every device (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › BabyLink).

Getting started

  1. On the phone near the baby, open BabyLink and tap Baby camera. A QR code appears.
  2. On your phone, tap Parent and scan the QR code.
  3. Done. Next time the devices find each other automatically.

To add more parents: open Devices and show the code to the new device.

The devices can’t find each other

  • Check that all devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Hotel and guest Wi-Fi often block devices from talking to each other. Use a Personal Hotspot from one of the iPhones instead.
  • Check that Local Network is allowed for BabyLink on every device.
  • Keep BabyLink open on the camera device.

I can’t hear anything

Sound starts muted on every connection, so phones near each other don’t whistle. Tap the sound button on the parent screen.

The video stops when the camera phone is locked

iOS stops the camera when the phone is locked. Keep BabyLink open on the camera phone; it dims its screen to black by itself. Connect it to a charger for the night.

Alerts during phone calls

If the camera phone gets a call, video may pause. BabyLink shows a “Call on the camera” alert. Tip: put the camera phone in Airplane mode with Wi-Fi on. BabyLink shows a short checklist.

Privacy

BabyLink has no account and no server. Video and sound go only between your devices and are never recorded. Read the Privacy Policy.

Important

BabyLink is not a medical device. It does not track breathing or prevent SIDS and does not replace adult supervision. Always follow safe sleep guidance.
