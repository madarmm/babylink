BabyLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

BabyLink turns two or more iPhones into a video baby monitor. It is made by Armen Madoyan, an individual developer in Berlin, Germany. Contact: a.madoyan@gmail.com

The short version

BabyLink does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Video and sound go directly between your own devices and are never recorded.

Camera and microphone

On the device you use as the camera, BabyLink uses the camera and the microphone only to stream live video and sound to the parent devices you have paired. The stream is sent directly between your devices over your Wi-Fi network or a Personal Hotspot and is encrypted (WebRTC, DTLS-SRTP). Nothing is recorded, saved or sent to the developer or to anyone else. iOS shows its own indicator while the camera or the microphone is in use.

Local network

BabyLink uses your local network (Bonjour and a direct connection between the devices) to find your paired devices and connect them. iOS asks you once to allow this. No internet connection and no server are used.

Data stored on your devices

BabyLink keeps only what it needs to work, on your devices:

  • the pairing with your other devices (a secret key, stored in the iPhone Keychain of each device);
  • device names you typed and the list of paired devices;
  • your settings: theme, language, alert and alarm settings, screen and notification options.

This data stays on your devices, is never sent to the developer, and is removed when you delete the app or use “Leave” / “Disconnect all”.

Notifications

Alerts (noise, movement, connection lost and the like) are local notifications created on your own device. No push notification service is used. A Noise or Movement notification can include the last picture from the camera; that picture is made on your device, is shown only in that notification (also on your lock screen, if you allow notifications there) and is not stored anywhere else. You can turn the picture off in the app’s settings.

Children

BabyLink is meant for parents and caregivers. It does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

BabyLink contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code and shares no data with third parties. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Your rights

Because the developer does not receive any personal data, there is no data to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions, write to a.madoyan@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If BabyLink changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
