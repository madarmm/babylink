BabyLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

BabyLink turns two or more iPhones into a video baby monitor. It is made by Armen Madoyan, an individual developer in Germany (Armen Madoyan, c/o Adressgeber #2358, An der Alten Ziegelei 38, 48157 Münster, Germany). Contact: babylink.support@gmail.com

The short version

BabyLink does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Video and sound go directly between your own devices and are never recorded.

Camera and microphone

On the device you use as the camera, BabyLink uses the camera and the microphone only to stream live video and sound to the parent devices you have paired. The stream is sent directly between your devices over your Wi-Fi network or a personal hotspot and is encrypted (WebRTC, DTLS-SRTP). Nothing is recorded, saved or sent to the developer or to anyone else. A visible indicator shows when the camera is streaming.

Local network

BabyLink uses your local network (Bonjour) to find your paired devices and connect them directly. No internet connection and no server are used.

Data stored on your devices

BabyLink keeps a few settings on each device: the pairing with your other devices, device names you typed, the list of paired devices, theme and alert settings. This data stays on your devices, is never sent to the developer, and is deleted when you delete the app or use “Leave” / “Disconnect all”.

Notifications

Alerts (for example noise, motion or connection lost) are local notifications created on your device. No push notification service is used. If “Photo in notifications” is on (you can turn it off in the parent settings), a movement or noise notification shows a still photo from the live video. The photo is made on your parent device, is visible on its lock screen like any notification, and is never sent anywhere.

Children

BabyLink is meant for parents and caregivers. It does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

BabyLink contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code and shares no data with third parties. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Your rights

Because the developer does not receive any personal data, there is no data to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions, write to babylink.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If BabyLink changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
