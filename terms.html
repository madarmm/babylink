What BabyLink is

BabyLink lets your own iPhones show you live video and sound from the room where your baby sleeps, over your Wi-Fi or a Personal Hotspot, and tells you about noise, movement and connection problems. It works only while the app is running on the devices and while they can reach each other.

Limits you should know

  • An alert can be late or missed. A device may be locked, switched off, out of range or out of battery; a Wi-Fi network may drop; iOS may silence or delay notifications; the alarm cannot be louder than your device’s volume.
  • iOS stops the camera when the camera device is locked. The sound continues; the video does not.
  • Hotel and guest networks often block devices from talking to each other.
  • Alerts are based on sound level and picture changes. They can be set off by other sounds and lights, and they can miss a quiet baby.

Check on your baby yourself. Keep the volume up, keep the devices charged and test the alarm (menu › Alarms › Test alarm) before relying on it.

Using BabyLink

Use BabyLink only to watch your own child or a child in your care, with the consent of the people who appear in the picture or can be heard, and in line with the laws where you are. Do not use it to watch other people.
