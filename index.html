BabyLink

Video baby monitor on the iPhones you already have.

One iPhone stays with the baby as the camera. Yours is the parent screen. Live video and sound go directly between your devices over your Wi-Fi or a Personal Hotspot.

No account. No internet. No server. No subscription. Nothing is recorded.

What it does

  • Pair once by scanning a code. Afterwards the devices find each other by themselves.
  • Up to 4 parent devices watch the same camera. Add, rename or remove them from any device.
  • Sound keeps playing with the screen locked or in other apps; picture in picture.
  • Noise and Movement alerts with the last picture in the notification. An alarm rings only when nobody reacts; you choose after how long.
  • BabyLink tells you when it cannot watch: connection lost, the camera device locked, the picture frozen, no sound.
  • Night mode, flashlight, front or back camera, audio only, dark camera screen – all from the parent device.
  • Camera battery, heat and incoming calls shown on the parent screen; a checklist before you leave the camera device.
  • Three dark themes; works turned on its side.

What you need

  • Two or more iPhones with BabyLink, on the same Wi-Fi or on one iPhone’s Personal Hotspot.
  • A charger for the camera device.
