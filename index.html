Good to know

What do I need?

Two or more devices with BabyLink on the same Wi-Fi, or on one phone's hotspot.

Does the camera device need to stay open?

Yes. The camera stops when the device is locked, so BabyLink keeps it open and dims its screen to black. Put it on a charger for the night.

Is anything stored or sent online?

No. There is no server and nothing is recorded. Read the Privacy Policy.
